What happens when someone calls

  1. It answers on the first ring

    Calls to your number forward to your AI number, all day, every day. Callers hear your greeting in a natural British voice, and they're told it's an AI assistant.

  2. It handles the call like your receptionist would

    It answers from the business information you give it, asks your questions to new enquiries, offers free times from your diary and puts urgent callers through to you.

  3. You get the result straight away

    A message, a booking or a scored lead lands in your inbox, with a summary and recording of the call in your dashboard.

Questions

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. The assistant introduces itself as a virtual assistant and says so honestly if asked. Most callers just want their question answered or their appointment booked, and it does that quickly.

Can I keep my existing phone number?

Yes. You get a new AI number, then set your current line to forward to it, either for all calls or only when you're busy or don't answer. Your phone provider can set this up, often with a short code dialled from your phone.

What if it can't answer a question?

It only says what's in the business information you give it. If it doesn't know, it takes a message or, if you've turned it on, puts the caller through to you.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are taking calls within an hour: choose a plan, get your number, describe your business and set up call forwarding. The dashboard walks you through each step.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time from the billing page in your dashboard. Plans are monthly with no minimum term.

Are calls recorded?

Yes, so you can listen back and see summaries in your dashboard. You should mention call recording and AI handling in your privacy notice; we can help with the wording.
